Privacy Policy

HypoDiabetic Monitor · Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information HypoDiabetic Monitor ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is developed by Inam Ur Rehman, Dublin, Ireland.

1. Information you enter

You may enter glucose readings, blood pressure readings, heart rate results, meals, steps, walks and app settings. This information is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. We do not have accounts, and we do not receive or keep a copy of this information on our servers.

2. Photos for meal and label scans

When you choose to scan a meal or a label, the photo is resized on your device and sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to our AI provider, Anthropic, PBC, to produce the nutrition or label estimate. Your condition setting (diabetes, hypertension or both) and any text you type about the meal are sent with it. We do not store the photo or the result on our server. Anthropic processes the request under its commercial terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Photos are only sent when you tap the analyze button.

3. Camera video for pulse check

The pulse check reads a short video you record or choose. The video is analyzed entirely on your device and is never uploaded.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your information for advertising.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts.

5. Hosting

The app is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Like any web host, Netlify may process technical data such as IP address and request time in server logs to deliver and secure the service.

6. Your choices and deleting data

You can delete individual entries in the Readings screen. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, uninstalling removes your data permanently.

7. Health information and medical disclaimer

HypoDiabetic Monitor is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Estimates from photos and the camera can be inaccurate. Always use your glucometer and blood pressure monitor for decisions and consult a healthcare professional.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have rights to access, correct, delete and object to processing of your personal data, and to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie). Since data is stored on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and the new version will be posted on this page.
